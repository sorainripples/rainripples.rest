On the Name
One day I felt crappy, so I sat by a Bayou. It was raining. Right by the willow trees, the lake rippled. Now, whenever I look at a lake, I think about it. It's in these quiet Moments, I reflect. When no one is watching.
RainRipples is the alias of Sabine. She's currently based in Toronto.
